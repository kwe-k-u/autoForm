Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoForm ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our browser extension, website, and related services (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide: your email address, persona (e.g. student, founder, opportunity seeker), age range, and any profile answers, essays, or application data you save so the Service can autofill and track your applications.
  • Application data: the answers, form fields, and documents the extension reads from or writes to application websites in order to autofill and track submissions on your behalf.
  • Usage data: information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited and features used, collected through analytics tools.
  • Device and technical data: browser type, general location (derived from IP address), and similar technical information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service, including autofilling and tracking applications
  • Generate AI-assisted drafts, suggestions, and templates for your applications
  • Communicate with you about the waitlist, product updates, and support requests
  • Monitor and analyze usage to improve performance and reliability
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service, such as hosting, database, analytics, and AI providers, under obligations to protect your data
  • The application websites you choose to submit to, since AutoForm autofills forms on those third-party sites at your direction
  • Legal authorities, where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy or a materially similar one

4. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data at any time as described in Section 7.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and extension may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the Service is used.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at kwekuappiah11@gmail.com. You can also uninstall the extension at any time to stop further data collection.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. International Users

If you access the Service from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in a different country with different data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at kwekuappiah11@gmail.com.
