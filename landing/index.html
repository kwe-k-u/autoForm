FAQs

What types of applications can I use AutoForm for?

AutoForm supports scholarship, school, incubator, and other opportunity applications. Our AI-powered tools adapt to your specific needs across 50+ countries.

Is AutoForm free to use?

Yes — the Free plan includes 1 saved profile and 10 saved answers at no cost. Paid plans start at $9 for 3 months of unlimited profiles and answers, with an Advanced plan at $29 that adds our opportunity newsletter and AI-drafted answers.

How does billing work?

Paid plans are a one-time payment for three months of access — not a recurring subscription. When your three months are up, you can manually renew whenever you're ready.

How is AutoForm different from other application tools?

AutoForm is built around two things: speed and consistency. It autofills applications faster using answers you've already given, then tracks every submission so your answers stay accurate wherever you apply.

How does the AI guidance work?

Our AI analyzes your application type and provides real-time feedback, suggestions, and tailored templates to help you craft compelling applications that maximize your chances of success.

Do you offer bundles for schools or incubators?

Yes — exam prep schools, startup incubators, and other opportunity programs can buy seat bundles for their participants. Contact sales for custom pricing.
